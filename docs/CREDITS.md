# Credits

Constellate and the hand-drawn stars are by Charlie Yates.

The eleven original star outlines in `assets/charlie-stars.json` came from Charlie's portfolio design. Their uneven point lengths and inner corners are adapted into editable 3–10-point constellations. Evenly spaced timing uses equal angles, so the initial five-point drawing is an adaptation of those traced silhouettes rather than a pixel-for-pixel copy.

The faint background uses the user-selected [GIFCities image](https://blob.gifcities.org/gifcities/7FROJKQWJY7Q63LO6A625Q4WBT4L6CAT.gif). Its six frames play at their original 50 ms timing, at 8% opacity beneath solid black text and star outlines. GIFCities is an Internet Archive project; the source artist and original license have not been identified. No ownership of that image is claimed.
